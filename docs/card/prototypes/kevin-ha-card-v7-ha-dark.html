Kevin card for Home Assistant, v6

Revised against Home Assistant's own card rules: the 12-column sections grid (56 px rows, 8 px gutter), 42 px feature rows, the theme font, standard confirmation and more-info dialogs,. HA default dark is the base theme. The card is a window with KEVIN® on a shelf: drag the sun to the moon for standby, read the subscription from a ring, hold a button to reboot. Scenes are shown, not started: the card tells you what is playing now and where it came from. The card shows what is playing and lets you change the preferences from onboarding: where KEVIN® lives, who lives there, how busy and how lively it is.

What is new

Everything on the card is live. Try the strip, the presets and the reboot.

Now playing, nothing to pressKEVIN® decides what to play from the preferences and the schedule. The card shows the current activity with a live waveform, its group, whether it is Adaptive or Basic Presence, and how long it has run. On standby it shows when Presence starts next.
Schedule you can read in a glanceSeven rows, one per day, with the Presence windows drawn as bars. Today is highlighted and the current hour is a thin line. Tap a day to see its hours. Edit stays in the Mitipi app, where the slot editor lives.

The gear opens the editor: environment select, lifestyle toggles (the list follows the environment), two sliders from 1 to 5. Day and night is the switchA 42 px feature row under the window. Drag the sun across, tap, or use the arrow keys. On is day, Standby is night, Unavailable is a grey unplugged window.
Sized for the sections gridOne layout, measured rather than guessed (toggle "HA grid" in the top bar to see the 56 px rows): a 12-column section and 13 rows with the preferences closed, 9 columns at its narrowest. Inside that box it reflows by its own width.
Standard dialogsReboot opens the HA confirmation dialog. Tapping the header or the tile body opens a more-info dialog with every attribute. Escape closes both.

Minimal, standard, full

Three variants, one data model. Minimal is the Home Assistant tile: one grid row, the icon circle switches On and Standby, the row opens more info, and a dot on the icon flags a subscription that needs attention. Standard is the everyday card. Full unfolds the detail standard keeps behind taps: hours per day on an hour ruler, the preferences editor open, and a device block with serial, firmware and reboot.

Widths

One layout, which reflows by its own width: under 320 px everything stacks, from 560 px the window moves to the left and the blocks pair up, from 860 px they sit in three columns. The widths below are real section widths, where a cell is about 30 px with an 8 px gap: a 12-column section is 448 px and a column_span 2 section is 904 px.
