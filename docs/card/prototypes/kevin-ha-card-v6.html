Kevin card for Home Assistant, v6

Revised against Home Assistant's own card rules: the 12-column sections grid (56 px rows, 8 px gutter), 42 px feature rows, the theme font, standard confirmation and more-info dialogs, and the tile anatomy for the compact layout. HA default dark is the base theme. The card is a window with KEVIN® on a shelf: drag the sun to the moon for standby, read the subscription from a ring, hold a button to reboot. Scenes are shown, not started: the card tells you what is playing now and where it came from. The card shows what is playing and lets you change the preferences from onboarding: where KEVIN® lives, who lives there, how busy and how lively it is.

What is new

Everything on the card is live. Try the strip, the presets and the reboot.

Now playing, nothing to pressKEVIN® decides what to play from the preferences and the schedule. The card shows the current activity with a live waveform, its group, whether it is Adaptive or Basic Presence, and how long it has run. On standby it shows when Presence starts next.
Schedule you can read in a glanceSeven rows, one per day, with the Presence windows drawn as bars. Today is highlighted and the current hour is a thin line. Tap a day to see its hours. Edit stays in the Mitipi app, where the slot editor lives.
Preferences behind a gearClosed, the card shows one line: where KEVIN® lives, who is there, Occupancy and Energy. The gear opens the editor: environment select, lifestyle toggles (the list follows the environment), two sliders from 1 to 5. Changes save to desired.customization on release, with a "Saving" marker until the device reports back. The gear is disabled while the device is unavailable.
Day and night is the switchA 42 px feature row under the window. Drag the sun across, tap, or use the arrow keys. On is day, Standby is night, Unavailable is a grey unplugged window.
Sized for the sections gridEach layout declares its size in HA grid units, measured, not guessed (toggle "HA grid" in the top bar to see the 56 px rows). Compact is a 6×1 tile, exactly 56 px. Standard takes a full 12-column section and 13 rows with preferences closed. Full is for a column_span 2 section, where it needs 7 rows.
Standard dialogsReboot opens the HA confirmation dialog. Tapping the header or the tile body opens a more-info dialog with every attribute. Escape closes both.

1. Themes

HA default dark first. The window keeps its own day and night colours in every theme, so the card is recognisable as KEVIN® wherever it sits. Everything else follows the HA theme variables.

2. Sizes and layouts

layout: compact | standard | full, each with its grid size (labels appear with "HA grid" on). Inside its box the card adapts to its own width: under 320 px one column, from 560 px the window on the left with blocks in pairs, from 860 px three columns. Widths below are real section widths: a cell is about 30 px with an 8 px gap, so 6 columns is 220 px, a 12-column section is 448 px, and a column_span 2 section is 904 px.

3. States

Every device, subscription and schedule situation, all in HA default dark. Cards are live.

4. Playground

Change theme, layout, width and subscription, and use the card.

5. Icon

Same icon set as v1, in icons/. The mono glyph follows the theme text colour, and the three tints below match the three window states.
